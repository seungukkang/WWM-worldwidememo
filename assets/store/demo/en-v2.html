A gentle introduction to diffusion models

6 min read · updated
Updated: this article was revised based on reader feedback.

Diffusion models learn to generate data by reversing a gradual noising process.

During training, Gaussian noise is added to an image step by step until nothing but noise remains.

A deep neural network is then trained to predict the noise that was injected at every step.

At sampling time, we start from pure noise and repeatedly remove the predicted noise.

Newer techniques such as distillation reduce this to just a handful of steps.
